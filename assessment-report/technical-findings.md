# Technical Findings

This document provides a detailed breakdown of the technical and organisational gaps identified during the NIS2 Gap Assessment. Each finding includes a description, impact, severity rating, and mapping to relevant NIS2 Articles.

---

## 1. Governance & Accountability (Article 20)

**Description:**  
The organisation has no defined cybersecurity roles, responsibilities, or accountability structure. Security ownership is unclear, and no formal governance model exists.

**Impact:**  
- No accountability for security failures  
- Slow decision-making during incidents  
- Lack of leadership oversight  

**Severity:** High  
**NIS2 Mapping:** Article 20 — Governance & Accountability

---

## 2. Risk Management (Article 21)

**Description:**  
There is no formal risk assessment methodology or documented risk management process. Risks are not identified, prioritised, or tracked.

**Impact:**  
- Inability to prioritise threats  
- No structured risk treatment  
- Increased exposure to unmanaged risks  

**Severity:** High  
**NIS2 Mapping:** Article 21 — Risk Management

---

## 3. Technical & Organisational Measures (Article 21)

**Description:**  
Essential cybersecurity controls are missing, including:  
- Logging & monitoring  
- Access control  
- Vulnerability management  
- Secure configuration  
- Encryption of sensitive data  

**Impact:**  
- High likelihood of undetected compromise  
- Increased attack surface  
- Non-compliance with NIS2 baseline security requirements  

**Severity:** Critical  
**NIS2 Mapping:** Article 21 — Technical & Organisational Measures

---

## 4. Incident Response (Article 23)

**Description:**  
The organisation lacks an incident response plan, detection capability, and reporting workflow. No procedures exist for escalation or regulatory notification.

**Impact:**  
- Delayed detection of cyber incidents  
- Failure to meet NIS2 reporting timelines  
- Increased operational disruption  

**Severity:** High  
**NIS2 Mapping:** Article 23 — Incident Reporting

---

## 5. Business Continuity & Crisis Management (Article 24)

**Description:**  
There is no business continuity plan (BCP), disaster recovery plan (DRP), or crisis communication strategy.

**Impact:**  
- Extended downtime during incidents  
- No structured recovery process  
- Poor communication with stakeholders  

**Severity:** Medium  
**NIS2 Mapping:** Article 24 — Business Continuity

---

## 6. Supply Chain Security (Article 26)

**Description:**  
No supplier risk assessments, monitoring, or contractual security requirements exist. Third-party risks are unmanaged.

**Impact:**  
- Exposure to supply chain compromise  
- No assurance of vendor security practices  
- Non-compliance with NIS2 supply chain obligations  

**Severity:** High  
**NIS2 Mapping:** Article 26 — Supply Chain Security
