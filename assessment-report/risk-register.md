# Risk Register
This risk register summarises all identified risks from the NIS2 Gap Assessment.  
Each risk includes likelihood, impact, severity, description, and recommended treatment.

---

## 📌 Risk Scoring Model
- **Likelihood:** Low / Medium / High  
- **Impact:** Low / Medium / High / Critical  
- **Risk Level:** Determined by Likelihood × Impact  
- **Treatment Options:** Mitigate / Accept / Transfer / Avoid

## 📑 Detailed Risk Descriptions

### **R-001 — Missing Governance Structure**
- **Issue:** No cybersecurity roles or responsibilities defined.  
- **Impact:** No accountability, slow incident response, unclear decision-making.  
- **Risk Level:** High  
- **Treatment:** Implement governance model and assign security ownership.

---

### **R-002 — No Risk Management Process**
- **Issue:** No formal risk assessment or risk register.  
- **Impact:** Organisation cannot prioritise threats or allocate resources.  
- **Risk Level:** High  
- **Treatment:** Adopt ISO 27005-aligned risk methodology.

---

### **R-003 — Missing Technical Controls**
- **Issue:** Logging, access control, vulnerability management, encryption missing.  
- **Impact:** High likelihood of undetected compromise.  
- **Risk Level:** Critical  
- **Treatment:** Implement baseline controls aligned with NIST CSF & ISO 27001.

---

### **R-004 — No Incident Response Plan**
- **Issue:** No IR plan, detection capability, or reporting workflow.  
- **Impact:** Delayed detection, regulatory non-compliance.  
- **Risk Level:** High  
- **Treatment:** Develop IR plan and reporting procedures.

---

### **R-005 — No Business Continuity Planning**
- **Issue:** No BCP/DRP or crisis communication strategy.  
- **Impact:** Extended downtime during incidents.  
- **Risk Level:** Medium  
- **Treatment:** Create BCP/DRP and run tabletop exercises.

---

### **R-006 — No Supplier Security Controls**
- **Issue:** No vendor vetting or contractual security requirements.  
- **Impact:** High exposure to supply chain compromise.  
- **Risk Level:** High  
- **Treatment:** Implement supplier risk assessments and security clauses.

---

## ✔ Summary
This risk register provides a structured view of all major risks impacting NIS2 compliance.  
It supports prioritisation, resource allocation, and long-term governance planning.
