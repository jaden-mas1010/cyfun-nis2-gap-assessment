# Executive Summary

This NIS2 Gap Assessment evaluates the cybersecurity maturity of a simulated financial services organisation. The assessment identifies critical gaps in governance, risk management, operational security, incident response, and supply chain security — all of which are required under the NIS2 Directive.

The organisation currently lacks formal cybersecurity governance, structured risk management, and essential technical controls. Incident response capabilities are minimal, and supplier security is not assessed or monitored. These gaps significantly increase exposure to cyber threats and regulatory penalties.

Key findings include:
- No defined cybersecurity roles or accountability structure.
- No formal risk assessment or risk management methodology.
- Missing essential controls such as logging, access control, vulnerability management, and encryption.
- No incident response plan or reporting workflow.
- No business continuity or crisis communication planning.
- No supplier vetting or contractual security requirements.

The organisation is at **high risk of non-compliance** with NIS2 Articles 20, 21, 23, 24, and 26. Immediate remediation is required to reduce operational and regulatory exposure.

This summary provides leadership with a high-level understanding of the organisation’s current cybersecurity posture and the urgent steps needed to achieve NIS2 readiness.
